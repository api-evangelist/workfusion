Financial crime investigators are hired to investigate. Yet much of their day can be consumed by something very different: searching systems, collecting data, assembling evidence, documenting findings and working through large volumes of alerts that ultimately prove non-suspicious. That challenge spans both Transaction Monitoring (TM) and Fraud.
